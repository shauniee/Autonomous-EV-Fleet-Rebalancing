# OneMap driving routes

A verified OneMap driving route is required for Dispatch approval. The default
[CRDS](crds.md) policy also requires OneMap route evidence when planning.

## Configure

Copy these entries from `.env.example` into your own `.env` and supply a
**OneMap REST access token** (the LTA AccountKey is unrelated):

```dotenv
ROUTING_PROVIDER=ONEMAP
ONEMAP_ACCESS_TOKEN=your-onemap-access-token
```

OneMap requires registration and token generation through its
[authentication API](https://www.onemap.gov.sg/apidocs/authentication). The REST
documentation states tokens expire after three days; replace expired tokens and
reload configuration. No email or password is stored by this scaffold.

From the repository root, run the full macOS startup between demos:

```bash
bash scripts/start-kubernetes.sh
```

It places routing configuration in `ev-fleet-routing`, referenced only by Dispatch.
The existing renderer accepts `--routing`; `--connect-only` never reads `.env` or
reloads configuration. Compose passes the same two variables only to Dispatch.
For direct Python execution, export the variables yourself. Tokens never enter
browser code, route records or log messages.

`ROUTING_PROVIDER=SIMULATED` permits local observation and legacy previews.
Dispatch refuses relocation approval without a verified OneMap route.

## Approved journey

With LTA facilities, the default **Automatic — parking or charging** mode chooses
a facility for each EV in the selected zone. Predicted arrival SOC below 35%
requires available charging; otherwise ordinary parking is selected. All trips
must preserve the operational reserve (20% by default). **Charging only** is an
explicit override. If no eligible EV/facility pair exists, the request remains
unfulfilled. OneMap road-distance checks on approval may reject an assignment
if it now needs charging but the proposed facility has no charger capacity.

Ground-team cable connection is assumed: simulated charging starts after verified
arrival/allocation and stops at 80%. There is no staff plug-in confirmation or
physical charger control.

1. Select a zone and request a recommendation. CRDS first screens facilities by
   approximate distance and capacity, then asks Dispatch for a OneMap estimate
   for each shortlisted EV and facility. Legacy previews use approximate corridors.
2. During approval, Dispatch obtains a fresh road route. It validates the geometry
   and rechecks the EV origin, current version, battery reserve and charging
   requirement. For CRDS, the final route also goes through benefit revalidation.
3. The command carries the immutable route. Fleet includes road distance in its
   atomic reserve check before claiming the car. No route credential is included.
4. Before its first telemetry in the OneMap relocation mode, each of the
   **20 moving EV simulators** calls Dispatch's internal `/api/v1/road-matches`
   endpoint. Dispatch asks OneMap to identify a routable start road within
   **200 m of its initial seed or last idle location**, failing closed if no
   reasonable road match exists. A new simulator must never road-match an
   active rental or unresolved dispatch; stored SOC is preserved. This idle
   placement occurs *before* a new simulated journey, not in transit.
5. With a parking or charging facility, Dispatch treats its geographical
   coordinate as an identity/visual reference, **not the physical arrival
   target**. It routes to the OneMap-provided final drivable road point,
   provided it lies within 150 m of the facility coordinate. The same road
   point is stored in the Fleet claim, simulator command and parking hold.
   The vehicle remains on the road when arriving or starting charging. No
   straight connector to the facility centre is animated or added to the
   route. Routes whose origin cannot be road-aligned or whose endpoint is
   too far from a facility are rejected **before any hold/claim**.
6. The simulator interpolates along OneMap's road vertices, consuming energy
   over road distance and using OneMap's journey duration as its average speed.
   A route starting more than 5 m from the already road-aligned EV is refused
   rather than showing a connector. It does not cut straight across corners.
7. The map selects a newly approved journey and highlights its road route, EV,
   start and destination. Choose another **OneMap journey**, click an active route
   or EV, or use **View route on map** in a relocation card. **Fit route** shows the
   whole journey; **Follow EV** centres the map as fresh telemetry arrives.
   **Routes** still controls overlay visibility. Selected completed routes remain
   available for review.
8. Below the map, view road distance, reported progress, estimated simulated
   travel time and expandable driving directions. The same details are available
   in relocation cards. EV positions come from Fleet telemetry; no browser-side
   movement or new route request is generated by the display controls.

## Limits and errors

- Travel times are OneMap estimates. Live-traffic adjustment has not been verified.
  The route ETA supplies the nominal speed. Simulated traffic can cap speed
  or stop movement on a covered segment. The issued route geometry stays fixed.
- The configured simulation clock accelerates journeys (10× by default). UI durations are simulated journey
  minutes, not a promised wall-clock arrival time. Remaining time is proportional
  to distance and cannot infer waiting time during communication failures.
- Tracking advances when telemetry arrives (five seconds by default, dashboard
  polling every two seconds). It is not continuous GPS tracking.
- The existing Fleet **arrival tolerance remains 50 m** (the
  `ARRIVAL_TOLERANCE_KM=0.05` safety setting is unchanged). It is checked
  relative to the real command's drivable road endpoint. Facility centre and
  road access position are deliberately distinct; parking verification uses
  that road access position rather than an off-road centre.
- The 200 m *idle road-match search radius* and 150 m *facility-to-road
  access search radius* are separate spatial admission checks, **not relaxed
  arrival tolerances**. They are simulation heuristics, not confirmation of
  a signed, legally accessible carpark driveway or EV charger connector.
- An off-road departure, missing road-access endpoint, or inconsistent road
  geometry fails closed (`ONEMAP_ORIGIN_NOT_ON_ROAD`,
  `ONEMAP_FACILITY_NO_ROAD_ACCESS`, or `ONEMAP_NO_SAFE_SPAWN_ROAD`).
  Legacy previews can first encounter road-access failures at approval. CRDS
  checks the route during planning and again at approval. Each approval is for
  the exact selected EV and facility.
- The **optional customer-rental workload** retains its separately modelled
  synthetic travel (straight-line movement); it is not presented as road-only
  OneMap navigation. This extension targets autonomous relocation and
  charging journeys by the 20 moving EV simulators.
- Missing/expired tokens, quota errors, invalid data and no-route responses produce
  explicit `ONEMAP_*` failure reasons before a new parking hold or vehicle claim.
  Such failures are rejected rather than retried by the reconciliation loop.
  Request a new recommendation after correcting configuration or an outage.
- Once issued, the route is fixed. Existing active-journey restart limitations
  remain; there is no new crash-recovery or production navigation guarantee.

The default provider setting is `SIMULATED`. Configure OneMap and reload the
Dispatch environment to exercise authenticated routing.
